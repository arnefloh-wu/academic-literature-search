# Journal quality flags

Record journal quality in the `rank` field as `AJG <grade>; VHB <grade>; FT50` (omit parts
that do not apply). The grades below are from memory of the Chartered ABS Academic Journal
Guide 2024 (AJG, formerly ABS), VHB-JOURQUAL 3 (German Academic Association of Business
Research) and the Financial Times FT50 list. **Verify a grade before relying on it**: search
"<journal> AJG 2024" or use harzing.com/resources/journal-quality-list (compiles AJG, VHB,
ABDC and others) and the VHB list at vhbonline.org. Where a grade is uncertain, write
`AJG 3 (verify)`.
